## Regions
**Regions** are the graphical reprensation of a filtering process of numeric values. Ranges of the targeted parameters were set to identify  supopulations. They are employed to obtain statistics on a selected ensemble of events as explained in the [**Show Statistics**]() function.
Regions are named with "r" followed by a two-digits index and will appear in the ImageJ ROI Manager. In AMICO, the region constraints are calculated on a graphical base, i.e. on a 0-1024 normalized interval, and are consequently linked to the adopted axis range. This means that the correct procedure to modify a region is the following:
- select the dotplot with the axis corresponding to the target parameters defined by the region
- ensure that the ranges (min-max) coincide with the original ones employed to create the region
- select the region of interest in the ROI manager and modify it
The procedure is identical both for rectangular or polygonal regions.

## Region List 
Regions are stored in an internal **Region List** reporting the interested parameters (X and Y axis), the relative ranges (min-max) and the X and Y arrays of coordinates defining the corresponding ImageJ selections.

## Gates
A **Gate** is a tool to focus on a target subpopulation. It employs the constraints defined by a region excluding from the population all the events not satisfying the set criteria that will disappear from the Dot Plot and will not counted in the statisical calculations.

<img width="1648" height="866" alt="immagine" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3fb101bf-4cd3-4b7f-afad-ba4108e57e60" />

Gates are activated by the dedicated **Gates:** (1) button in the main window.

It's possible to build a gate by a logical combination of the regions criteria inserting the definition in the window activated by the **Set Gate** (2) button according to the following rules:
- region names must follow the adopted naming convention (r + two digits index)
- the logical operators must be written in capital letters (AND, OR). ! stands for the NOT operator
- use ( to define the correct orders of the logical operations

The gate expression will appear in the list of possible gates displayed on pressing the **Gates** (1) button.

NB. A wrong expression will be anyway displayed in the list. Its selection will cause macro aborting for an internal error.

## Subcompartment Gates
Gates in the subcompartment are identical to the ones defined for the main **Cell** compartment. Regions (indicated by a prefix that coincides with the identificative index of the subcompartment) can be logically combined as usual. Gates operating on the **Cells** are also effective at this level eliminating all the objects contained in a cell that does not satisfy the coded requirements.
Gating in the subcompartment has obviously an impact on the main Cell Compartment and on the other subcompartments. These effects are controlled by the **"Active on other compartments" (1)** option:
- if unchecked the gate will act in the subcompartment of origin as usual excluding objects not satisfying the gate conditions. In the main **Cell** compartment the event will be excluded from the cell of origin thus altering the derived properties (i.e. number of objects in the cell, mean, total and fractional values)
- if checked the **Cell** containing the event will be excluded from the population thus affecting also the other subcompartments
