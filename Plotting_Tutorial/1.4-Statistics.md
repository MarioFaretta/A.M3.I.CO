Once identified a targeted subpopulation by regions and gates, it's possible to obtain a quantitative description by looking at the mean values of the  measured parameters. In every compartment, every region can be included or not (**"Not Calculated"** option) in the statistics calculation. Besides statistics, a selected number of representative images of the cells in the region can be displayed.
