Once identified a targeted subpopulation by regions and gates, it's possible to obtain a quantitative description by looking at the mean values of the  measured parameters. In every compartment, regions can be included or not (**"Not Calculated"** choice. Choose "0" to obtain statistics with no representative images) in the statistics calculation. 

<img width="714" height="855" alt="immagine" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6943af64-ab7f-47fe-9b47-8bd4aea1fc48" />

The calculated values are visualized in the ImageJ Results Window:

<img width="1349" height="917" alt="immagine" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6412e786-9523-4c46-9daf-8cfa8856e89e" />

The "All" column refers to the whole population. If a gate is active the statistics will be calculated only on the events that satisfies the set conditions. 
- Number of Events
- % of cells in the region calculated over all the events detected in the analysis (Total)
- & of cells in the region calculated over the events in the gate (Gated)
- Mean value of all the defined parameters

## Images

Besides statistics, a selected number of representative images of the cells in the region can be displayed.

<img width="2000" height="1111" alt="immagine" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/30e7a41c-45aa-4ba3-995c-90ffab25b6d3" />

Each stack contains the previously selected channels to be displayed plus the progressive cell index, the **Cell** and the **Subcompartment** masks. The Z value points to the different cell or originating image in the 5D Single cell or Whole Field Gallery respectively. The View Factor specify the extension of the region around the selected cell.
