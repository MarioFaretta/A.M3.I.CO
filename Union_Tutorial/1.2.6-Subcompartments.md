## Subcompartment Settings

<img width="843" height="984" alt="immagine" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e125a398-14c5-4aad-b663-e7ca8bf4092d" />

Insert the name of the compartment and specify with the **Whole Cell Subcompartment** option if it's limited to nuclei (unchecked) or extended to the whole cell region (checked).

The **Distnce Measurement (Border)** option, if checked, allows to calculate the distance from the main mask borders (e.g. Nuclei) of every detected object in the subcompartment.
