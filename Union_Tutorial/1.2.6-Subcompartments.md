## Subcompartment Settings

<img width="843" height="984" alt="immagine" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e125a398-14c5-4aad-b663-e7ca8bf4092d" />

Insert the name of the compartment and specify, with the **Whole Cell Subcompartment** option, if it's limited to nuclei (unchecked) or extended to the whole cell region (checked).

The **Distance Measurement (Border)** option, if checked, allows to calculate the distance from the main mask borders (e.g. Nuclei) of every detected object in the subcompartment.

It is then possible to specify the targeted channel for the calculation of their fluorescence values.

<img width="959" height="714" alt="immagine" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ae56d035-a3c7-4dda-9df0-c820811e390a" />


Beside the obvious choice for the originating channel, the intensity of the various channels in the segmented masks of the subcompartment may provide:

- a measure of colocalization by providing the amount of a second marker contained in the identified structure, as in the case of e.g. measurement of the recruitment of targeted proteins inside foci, vesicles...
  
- a way to localize structures intracellularly e.g. analysis of the intensity of the marker identifying a cell compartement (nucleus, cytoskeleton) can provide a measure of the relative positioning. High or low values of the marker reveal if the objects  in the segmented subcompartment are positioned respectively in or out the marked intracellular compartment as in the case of DNA stain dyes (High=Nuclear; Low=Cytoplasmic)

As for main objects/cells, specification of the Min/Max Size is requested

<img width="1988" height="455" alt="immagine" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e123551a-058f-4d08-a0e8-a46f04fb11e7" />


The macro proceeds on the segmentation of the image, produces the corresponding list of recognized ohjects in the reference channel in the ROI manager in ImageJ and then generate a series of filtered images of the channel selected for the subcompartment identification:

- **No Filtering** (Slice 1): background subtracted image
- **Laplace of Gaussian** (Slice 2): the background-subtracted image is subjected  to a Convolution with a classical Laplace of Gaussian Kernel to highlight spotted structures
- **Top-Hat** (Slice 3): Minimum-Maximum Filtering of the background subtracted channel image. Again useful for the detection of spots
- **Variance** (Slice 4): ImageJ Variance Filter to enhance structure borders
- **RollingBall Spot** (Slice 5): RollingBall command calculated according to the minimum object-size in the subcompartment
