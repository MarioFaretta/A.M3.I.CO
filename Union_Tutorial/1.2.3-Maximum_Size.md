## Maximum Size

<img width="1264" height="979" alt="immagine" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a384adfa-96da-404a-96f9-235112963690" />

To define the maximum size for the segmented objects:
- Enter the value in pixels of the area of the largest cell to be considered (e.g. you can decide to consider or not cell aggregates not correctly processed by the Watershed Algorithm
- If you prefer, check the "Get from ROI" option and then draw a region on the targeted largest object in the image
