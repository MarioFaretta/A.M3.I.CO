NB. The term **Cell** from now on will indicate the objects in the highest position in the hierarchy of identified objects in the image. It will not necessarily coincide with real "biological" cells but it can be referred to every targeted structure to be recognized and counted in the images. Cells can thus be Whole cells, Cores of a TMA, Organoids, intracellular organelles (e.g. mithocondria, vesiscles, nuclear bodies), or in-vitro created structures as beads, nanoparticles and so on. Inside cells, it is possible to identify inner objects named **Subcompartment**. Similarly to the above mentioned examples, subcompartments can coincide with intracellular structures, cells, or everything else spatially contained in the cell object.

# Subtract background

The next step is the setting of the Background Subtraction Mode

<img width="1038" height="457" alt="immagine" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/88411b3b-db88-4b04-82b5-83b5d445effe" />

Among the options:

- **None**: No background subtraction  
- [**RollingBall**](1.2.1-RollingBall_Background_Subtraciontraction.md): Local subtraction algorithm employing the homonimous command in ImageJ.  
- **Flatfield (Deprecated)**: Flatfield and background correction algorithm. Not working in this version. Under revision.
    
Two additional options can be checked:
  
- **Apply Watershed to separate clusters**: employs the ImageJ **"Watershed"** command to separated adjacent cells. The performances strongly depends on the employed cells shape
- **Fill internal holes**: fill internal zero regions inside the cell masks. Its use obviously depends on the objects to be segmented. If nuclei are segmented, nucleoli will be filled. This way it is possible to decide if the nucleolus fluorescence in all the channels must be considered (Checked) or not (Unchecked) in the computing. Same reasoning if cytoplasmic stains are used as cell identifiers: nuclear fluorescence can be included (Checked) or not (Unchecked) from the calculus of mean and integrated intensities for every channel.

NB. If the target folder contains single-tiff images, one per channel, the entered number will refer to the number  n of different positions, i.e. the number of processed files will be n * number of channels.

The macro will produce a tab-text file containing one row per every identified objects, reporting the calculated values in the corresponding columns. The prefix 0: indicates the Main Compartment in the hierarchy of the detected objects. As mentioned above,  a **Cell** can be a real cell or another main object in the analysis - single cores in a Tissue Micro Array, cell spheroids,... and then looking at single cells as a subcompartment. Prefixes greater than 0 mark the different subcompartments. 

    1. FilePath: Index of the position of the file of origin in the analyzed image list
    2. X: X Coordinates of the masks of the cell and of the subcompartment objects contained in the cell
    3. Y: Y Coordinates of the masks of the cell and of the subcompartment objects contained in the cell
    Physical Parameters    
    4. 0: Cell_N.: Progressive Index identifying the cell
    5. 0: Cell_Xc: X coordinate of the Centroid 
    6. 0: Cell_Yc: Y coordinate of the Centroid
    7. 0: Cell_Area: Area of the Cell in pixels (i.e. number of pixels contained in the segmented mask)
    8. 0: Cell_Circularity: Shape factor reflecting the "Roundness" of the Cell
    Fluorescence Parameters (3 columns * number of channels)
    9. 0: Cell_Mean[Ch..]: Mean Intensity value of the channel Ch.. calculated over the background subtracted intensities of the pixels contained in the segmented mask
    10. 0: Cell_Total Intensity[Ch..]: Integrated Intensity value of the channel Ch.. calculated over the background subtracted intensities of the pixels contained in the segmented mask (i.e. Mean * Area)
    11. 0: Cell_StdDev[Ch..]: Standard Deviation of the channel Ch.. calculated over the background subtracted intensities of the pixels contained in the segmented mask
    Recap values relative SubcompX referred to the corresponding cell. The heading containg the name of the Subcomp (SubCompX) followed by the originating channels (ChY)
    12. 0: Cell_Number (SubcompX(ChY)): Number of objects of the SubcompX contained in the cell
    13. 0: Cell_Average Size (SubcompX(ChY)): Average Size of all the subcomp objects contained in the cell
    14. 0: Cell_Area Fraction (SubcompX(ChY)): Percentage of the cell area occupied by the subcomp objects
    15. 0: Cell_Mean[Ch..] (SubcompX(ChY)): Mean Intensity of the subcomp objects contained in the cell. It's an average calculated over objects not over pixels
    16. 0: Cell_Total Intensity[Ch..] (SubcompX(ChY)): Sum of the Intensities of all the subcomp objects contained in the cell
    17. 0: Cell_Fractional Intensity[Ch..]  (SubcompX(ChY)): Percentage of the subcomp [Ch..] total intensity with respect to the total intensity of the cell
    Subcomp Values reference columns (marked by the progressive number of subcompartment e.g. 1:). Every column contains a | separated vector reporting the parameter value for the corresponding indexed object in the cell that will be exploded when loaded for the statistical analysis. These values allow to operate on the subcomp objets independently of the originating cell. The following columns will be repeated for every subcomptarment with a progressive indexing.
    Physical Parameters
    18. 1: SubcompX(ChY)_# spot: Number of objects of the SubcompX objects contained in the cell
    19. 1: SubcompX(ChY)_XM: X coordinates of the centre of the SubcompX objects contained in the cell (vector)
    20. 1: SubcompX(ChY)_YM: Y coordinates of the centre of the SubcompX objects contained in the cell (vector)
    21. 1: SubcompX(ChY)_Area Spot: Area of the SubcompX objects contained in the cell (vector)
    22. 1: SubcompX(ChY)_Circ Spot: Circularity of the SubcompX objects contained in the cell (vector)
    Fluorescence Parameters (3 columns * number of channels)
    23. 1: SubcompX(ChY)_MeanSpot[Ch..]: Mean Intensity value of the channel Ch.. calculated over the background subtracted intensities of the pixels contained in the segmented masks of the subcomp (vector)
    24. 1: SubcompX(ChY)_IntDemSpot[Ch..]: Integrated Intensity value of the channel Ch.. calculated over the background subtracted intensities of the pixels contained in the segmented masks of the subcomp (i.e. Mean * Area) (vector)
    25. 1: SubcompX(ChY)_StDevSpot[Ch..]: Standard Deviation of the channel Ch.. calculated over the background subtracted intensities of the pixels contained in the segmented masks of the subcomp (vector)

The file will be closed by a terminating row reporting the total number of analyzed objects (cells and subcomp) in the corresponding columns.
Under the  closing row the directory containing the analyzed images will be reported, followed line by line by the parameters employed in the analysis
