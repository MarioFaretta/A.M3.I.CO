## Threshold

To select the more efficient threshold option

<img width="1271" height="978" alt="immagine" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c0e5341e-1e6e-4187-af62-a1920dcad281" />

test the options by selecting one voice from the Drop-down menu in the Threshold window and verify the effect on the displayed image. In general, for nuclei segmentation, the **Huang** and **Li** options work quite well. **Otsu** is another well performing option a little bit more restrictive (i.e. in presence of heterogenously stained nuclei induced by different chromatin conformation, fainter cells can be partially segmented with unrecognized parts).

Once you have found the best performing threshold press ok on the window and enter your choiches in the **Auto Thresholding** window. To reduce the extension of the segmented mask an **Erosion Factor** can be introduced corresponding to the number of pixels eroded from the segmented cell masks (the value 0 is for no erosion).
